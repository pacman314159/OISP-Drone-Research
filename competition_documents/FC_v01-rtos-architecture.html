FC_v01 — RTOS Flight-Control Architecture

Verified against the live firmware tree, 20 November 2026. Five layers, 14 resident FreeRTOS tasks plus 5 workers that exist only while the flight state machine keeps them alive, and every cross-task hand-off carried by one Layer-3 buffer, queue or notification.

ESP32-S3 · 2 × LX7 @ 240 MHz FreeRTOS SMP · 1 kHz tick 19 tasks · 14 resident 19 ring buffers · 8 streams 4 queues · 5 mutexes no control law yet

Scheduling model

  • FreeRTOS SMP, 1 kHz tick. Four priority levels only: P1 low, P2 medium, P3 high, P4 realtime (motor alone).
  • Every periodic loop uses vTaskDelayUntil with an overrun guard: more than two periods late resets the phase instead of catching up.
  • All 19 task definitions are tskNO_AFFINITY — two cores, one scheduler, no pinning.
  • Stacks 2–20 KB; CALIB_MAG needs 20 KB for its 1000-sample cloud. No dynamic allocation on any flight path.

IPC contract

  • 19 RingBuffer instances across 8 streams, depth 10 (≈ 20 ms of 500 Hz data), single-producer / single-consumer, overwrite-on-full.
  • The primitives are guarded by the global sys_spinlock; consumers use front() or get_snapshot(), so no task blocks another on the flight path.
  • 4 queues: motor 4, LED 16, calibration report 16, OTA 32 (created only for an OTA session).
  • 5 mutexes: i2c0 20 ms, spi_bus 20 ms, flash, ekf_state 5 ms, i2c1 (reserved, unused).

Authority & safety

  • Five pre-flight gates — sensor-stream health, stationary, passive calibration, tilt, RC link. Any failure lands in DEVELOPMENT, a terminal state.
  • Emergency path bypasses the queue: xQueueSendToFront(EMERGENCY_STOP) plus a direct xTaskNotify(bit 0); MOTOR disarms on its next 250 Hz tick.
  • CALIB stays terminal until reboot by design (0040), so stale calibration cannot leak into a flight loop.
  • L5 owns every lifecycle action — spawn, suspend, halt. Workers never suspend or delete themselves (0042).

Honest scope

  • No cascaded PID and no INDI controller: src/core/control/ is a reservation comment.
  • MOTOR arms, disarms and supervises; the mixer that would consume indi_output is commented out.
  • BMP280 / MS5611, a GNSS position EKF and the STM32 target are planned, not present.
  • The blueprint's .md twin is 0 bytes here, so the PDF was used as the claim source.
