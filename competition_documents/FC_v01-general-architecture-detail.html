FC_v01 Flight Controller — General Architecture

Five-layer FreeRTOS firmware on one ESP32-S3: strict downward-only dependencies, 14 resident tasks plus 5 on-demand workers, hardware SIMD through a Layer-1 compute HAL, and a 500 Hz sensing/estimation chain. The control law is intentionally absent — this figure shows what the tree implements, not the intended end state.

verified against source · 2026-11-20

What the numbers mean

Hz is the nominal loop rate (vTaskDelayUntil), pN the FreeRTOS priority (4 = highest). Rates, priorities and stack sizes are read from include/config.h; they are nominal constants, not measurements.

What is missing on purpose

No mixer, no PID, no INDI, no attitude setpoint and no power telemetry exist in the tree. The 500 Hz chain terminates at state estimation; the 250 Hz motor task only arms and disarms the ESCs.
